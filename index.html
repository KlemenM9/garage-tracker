Install Digital Garage Manager

Direct Browser Install Ready
Click to install directly to your device right now.
On Your Samsung S25 Ultra (Android)
  1. Open https://klemenm9.github.io/garage-tracker/ in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  4. Confirm Install — the Digital Garage Manager icon will appear on your phone's home screen and work offline!
On Your Windows / Mac PC
  1. Open https://klemenm9.github.io/garage-tracker/ in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look at the right side of the address bar (URL bar) at the top of the browser.
  3. Click the Install icon (a small monitor with a down arrow ) and click Install.
  4. It will open in its own standalone window and place a shortcut on your Desktop / Start Menu.
