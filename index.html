Engine Oil & Filter Change (1.5 TSI DPCA)

0/10 Steps Checked
Tightening Torques & Specs Oil Sump Drain Plug: 30 Nm (new washer) • Spin-on Oil Filter: 25 Nm • Undertray T25 screws: 2 Nm / M6 bolts: 6 Nm
Required Tools & VAG Special Tools 19mm socket, 76mm 14-flute oil filter cup wrench, Torx T25 & T30, Torque wrench (5–50 Nm), 4.3L VW 508 00 (0W-20) oil
Warm engine to normal operating temperature (~60°C oil temp), switch ignition OFF, and raise vehicle safely on lift or jack stands.
Open the bonnet and loosen the upper oil filler cap so the crankcase vents cleanly during draining.
Remove the lower noise insulation undertray by unscrewing the Torx T25 perimeter screws and the rear T30 bolts.
Position drain pan beneath the steel oil sump. Remove the M14x1.5 drain plug and allow the old oil to drain completely (at least 10 minutes).
Unscrew the spin-on oil filter from the lower front of the engine block using a 76mm filter socket. Clean the sealing flange on the block.
Lightly oil the rubber seal of the new OEM oil filter (04E 115 561 H / MANN W 712/95), thread it on by hand, and torque to 25 Nm.
Install a new drain plug (or new captive seal ring) into the sump and tighten to 30 Nm.
Pour in 4.0 Litres of VW 508 00 (0W-20) oil first. Start the engine and idle for 30 seconds until the oil pressure warning light goes out.
Switch off engine, wait 3 minutes for oil to settle, check the dipstick, and top up to the top of the hatched zone (~4.3 Litres total).
Inspect drain plug and filter for leaks, reinstall the lower undertray, and reset the Service Reminder via the infotainment Vehicle Settings > Service menu.

7-Speed Wet-Clutch DSG (0GC / DQ381 Code TVE) Fluid & Filter Service

0/11 Steps Checked
Tightening Torques & Specs Main Gearbox Plug: 45 Nm • Internal Plastic Overflow Pipe: 3 Nm • Mechatronic Drain Plug (Single-Use): 20 Nm + 45° • Filter Cap: 20 Nm
Required Tools & VAG Special Tools 14mm hex bit, 8mm hex bit, Torx T45, 24mm socket, VAS 6262A filling adapter, OBD temperature tool, 6L VW G 055 529 A2 DSG fluid
Ensure vehicle is completely level on a lift or 4 jack stands. Remove the air filter intake ducting (and battery tray if replacing the upper cartridge filter) to access the 24mm plastic DSG filter housing cap on top of the 0GC gearbox.
Loosen the 24mm upper DSG oil filter housing cap a few turns first so air can enter the housing and drain oil back into the sump, then replace the filter element and new O-ring and torque cap to 20 Nm.
Remove the lower engine undertray. Locate BOTH drain points on the bottom of the 0GC (DQ381) gearbox: the main gearbox inspection/level plug (14mm hex) and the separate Mechatronic unit drain plug (Torx T45).
Remove the 14mm main gearbox inspection plug (some oil will trickle out). Then insert an 8mm hex key inside the hole to unscrew the white plastic internal overflow pipe—approximately 4.0 to 4.5 Litres of fluid will drain.
Remove the Torx T45 Mechatronic drain plug and allow the remaining ~1.2 Litres of mechatronic fluid to drain completely.
Install a BRAND NEW single-use Mechatronic drain plug (comes with integrated seal) and tighten to 20 Nm + 45° additional turn.
Screw the white plastic overflow pipe back into the main drain hole and tighten gently to 3 Nm.
Thread the VAS 6262A filling adapter into the main inspection hole and pump in 6.0 Litres of fresh VW G 055 529 A2 DSG fluid.
With the filling hose still attached, start the engine, press the brake pedal, and cycle the selector through P - R - N - D - S holding each position for 5 seconds, then return to P.
Monitor Gearbox Fluid Temperature via OBD: once temperature reaches between 30°C and 40°C (with engine idling in P), disconnect the filler hose and let excess fluid drain down to a steady drip.
Before temperature exceeds 45°C, install the main M24 inspection plug with a new aluminium seal ring and torque to 45 Nm.

Spark Plugs Replacement (1.5 TSI EA211 evo DPCA)

0/7 Steps Checked
Tightening Torques & Specs Spark Plugs (M12x1.25): 22 Nm • Ignition Coil Retaining Bolts: 8 Nm • Charge Air Pipe Fasteners: 7 Nm
Required Tools & VAG Special Tools Puller T10530, 14mm thin-wall magnetic spark plug socket, Torque wrench (5–30 Nm), Dielectric silicone grease (G 052 565 A1)
Ensure the 1.5 TSI aluminum cylinder head is cool to the touch before loosening spark plugs to prevent thread galling.
Release the clips and move the upper charge-air pipe / intake resonator aside for clear access to cylinders 1 to 4.
Unclip the electrical harness connectors from all 4 pencil ignition coils and unscrew the M6 retaining bolts (10mm / Torx T30).
Attach puller tool T10530 to each ignition coil and pull straight upward—never pry with a screwdriver or the rubber boot will tear inside the well.
Blow out each spark plug well with compressed air so zero grit falls into the combustion chamber.
Unscrew the old spark plugs using a 14mm thin-wall socket. Thread the new OEM plugs (05E 905 602) in by hand first, then torque strictly to 22 Nm.
Apply a thin bead of VW silicone paste (G 052 565 A1) inside the bottom lip of each ignition coil boot, press coils firmly onto the plugs, and torque the M6 coil bolts to 8 Nm.

Dust & Pollen Cabin Air Filter Replacement

0/5 Steps Checked
Tightening Torques & Specs Tool-free snap clips behind passenger glovebox
Required Tools & VAG Special Tools OEM Activated Carbon / Biofunctional Filter (5Q0 819 653 / 5Q0 819 669), Vacuum cleaner
Open the front passenger glovebox on your Cupra Formentor and empty its contents.
Press the two plastic retaining tabs on the upper inside walls of the glovebox inward, then gently release the damping rod on the right side so the glovebox swings downward.
Pinch the three top locking tabs on the rectangular HVAC filter cover lid and pull the cover forward.
Slide out the old cabin filter element and vacuum any leaves out of the blower housing.
Insert the new activated charcoal filter with the AIRFLOW arrow pointing DOWNWARD toward the floor, snap the cover closed, and re-engage the glovebox damper.

Engine Air Filter Element Replacement (1.5 TSI)

0/4 Steps Checked
Tightening Torques & Specs Air Filter Upper Housing Screws (7x Torx T25): 1.5 Nm
Required Tools & VAG Special Tools Torx T25 driver, OEM Air Filter 5Q0 129 620 B, Shop vacuum
Unscrew the 7 captive Torx T25 screws around the perimeter of the upper air filter housing.
Lift the upper housing cover carefully without straining the attached vacuum hoses and remove the old pleated air filter element.
Vacuum out all sand and debris from the lower airbox chamber and clean the lower snow grate.
Seat the new filter element cleanly into the perimeter groove, close the lid, and tighten the T25 screws evenly to 1.5 Nm.
